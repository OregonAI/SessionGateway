# The snapshot carries each Agency's parent, and an Agency the Chapter table lists for a chapter also gives its parent a derived link through it

ADR-0012 left this open: the Chapter table lists the sub-unit whose rules implement a chapter, never its Department, and a Fiscal Impact Statement that names "Department of Human Services" resolves to the Department. So the two halves of spec #1's question (a), "touches statutes my Agency administers" and "names my Agency in a Fiscal Impact Statement", answered under different ids for any Agency with sub-units. The review of ticket #16 reproduced it: `find_measures` for `department-of-human-services` listed HB 3795's nodes under "touches its statutes" and none of SB 69's, whose chapter 659A the table gives to `department-of-human-services-aging-and-people-with-disabilities-and-developmental-disabilities` alone. A bill amending ORS chapter 419B would never have been in the Department's statutes group, whatever its Fiscal Impact Statement said. ADR-0012 named the fix: the registry records the parent in each row's `relations`, and the snapshot did not copy it.

**The snapshot carries `parent`.** Each row of the snapshot's `agencies` has `parent`, the slug of the body the registry places it under, or null. The build reads it from `relations` in `_meta/catalog/agencies.yml`: the target of the row's relation, whatever its kind (`part_of`, `administered_by` or `undetermined`). Measured on 2026-10-30 against ERF at commit 58e1839: 82 of the 190 rows have one relation (33 `part_of`, 46 `administered_by`, 3 `undetermined`; the three sources are `oar-index` 80, `registry` 1 and `statute` 1), 108 have none, no row has more than one, no target is missing from the registry, and no parent has a parent of its own. The lead's decision was to take the relation, not to sort the relations by kind, so a board that is `administered_by` a Department gives the Department a link, as a division that is `part_of` it does. The file's `formatVersion` stays 1: no snapshot has been deployed, and a file built before this ADR fails to parse, naming `agencies.N.parent`.

- A row the registry places under **more than one body** gets no parent, and the build says so on stderr, naming the row and the bodies. ERF keeps such a disagreement (its `relations` doc says the OAR index, DAS and a statute may place a body under different parents) and treats it as a finding; this build takes no side. The registry has no such row today, so a test makes one up.
- A relation whose target is **not a row of the registry** stops the build, naming the row and the target, as a rule of an OAR chapter the registry lacks does (ADR-0012): the link through it would name an Agency that does not exist. A row with no `relations` key at all also stops the build: ERF writes `relations` on every row, and a row that has not said is not a row with no parent.
- The **trimmed fixture** keeps the parent of every Agency it keeps (and a parent's own parent), whether or not a text names the parent or a rule of its own implements a kept chapter, so that no `parent` names an Agency the snapshot lacks. All 55 parents of the 114 kept rows were already kept, so the fixture's rows did not change but for the new field. `verify.ts` checks the rule without a checkout.

**A derived link to a sub-unit also gives its parent a derived link.** For each chapter that a Statute Link which changes the law reaches, each Agency the table lists for the chapter gets the link (ADR-0027), and the parent of each of them gets one too, unless the table lists the parent for the chapter itself. Its evidence is the same chapter, Statute Link and count, and `through`, the sub-units the table lists for the chapter under that parent, in the table's order: one link for each parent and chapter, however many of its sub-units implement it. The store keeps all of them. The answer shows the first two with `through_total`, because a Department can have many (Agriculture has 24 sub-units in the registry, 22 of them implementing chapter 192) and a page of `find_measures` is bounded (ADR-0028). A parent the table lists for the chapter keeps the link the table gives it, with no `through`, because its own rules implement the chapter and the link needs no explaining. The sub-unit keeps its own link: its Agency Link is a fact about the sub-unit, and the parent's is the Department's reading of it. Only one level is followed, because the registry has none deeper.

The parent gets the link of a **derived** kind only. A sub-unit named in the text or in a Fiscal Impact Statement, or whose Testimony is submitted, does not give its parent a link: those are statements about the sub-unit, and the extractor already resolves the Department's own name to the Department.

Measured on the fixture on 2026-10-30: SB 69's A-Engrossed Version had derived links to 7 Agencies and has 10, the three added being the Departments of three sub-units (DAS's Chief Human Resources Office, DHS's Aging and People with Disabilities and Developmental Disabilities, OHA's Public Employees Benefit Board). `find_measures` for `department-of-human-services` lists SB 69's four nodes (A-Engrossed, Enrolled, -1 and -2) with HB 3795's five under "touches its statutes", each of SB 69's naming `through` the sub-unit; its Fiscal Impact Statement group is unchanged.

## Considered Options

- **Find the parent when the server answers**, from the registry in the database. The server would need the registry and the Chapter table, which ADR-0012 and ADR-0027 keep in the worker; and the Agency Link stored for the sub-unit is what the worker already has.
- **Give a parent the links of every sub-unit, of every kind.** A sub-unit named in the text is not a statement about the Department.
- **Follow only `part_of` relations.** Narrower, but a Department that administers a board implements that board's statutes, and the lead's decision was the registry's relations. The kind is not copied, so the choice can be changed in the build without a change to the format.
- **Copy the relation kind into the snapshot.** Nothing reads it yet.
- **A link for each sub-unit under a parent.** The Department's evidence would grow with its sub-units for no new fact; `through` lists them.

## Consequences

- A snapshot has to be rebuilt to carry `parent`. A database built from an older snapshot reads its Agency Links once, at its snapshot (ADR-0027); the worker now reads them again when the snapshot's version changes (ADR-0027, "A snapshot change").
- A Department's "touches its statutes" group grows by the Measures that change a chapter only a sub-unit implements. Chapter 192 (public records) is listed for 57 Agencies in the fixture, and five Departments (Consumer and Business Services, Energy, Human Services, the Department of Education and the Secretary of State) are the parents of some of them and not listed themselves: a Measure that amends a section of it now gives those five a link too, through the sub-units, beside the 57.
- Two Agencies can carry the same evidence for a document, the sub-unit and its parent. `find_measures` for the Department shows `through`, so an agent can tell the Department's own chapter from its sub-unit's.
