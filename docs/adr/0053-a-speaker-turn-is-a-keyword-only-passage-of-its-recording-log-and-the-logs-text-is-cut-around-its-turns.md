# A speaker turn is a Keyword-only Passage of its Recording Log, and the log's text is cut around its turns

ADR-0052 stored a log's speaker turns in a table of their own and deferred to ticket #127 making each turn a Keyword-only Passage of its log, so that `search` finds a turn through the keyword path it already has and needs no second path for turns. Spec #123 stage 1 asks for the rest: `search` returns a turn as a Hit with its speaker, Meeting and timecode, and `get_measure` lists the hearings at which a Measure was taken up. The decisions are how the log's text is cut, how a Passage is linked to its turn, what a turn Hit is, and how a database that has the index gets the Passages.

## Decision

- **The log's passages replace its undifferentiated ones, and every line of the text is in exactly one.** A log with an index is cut around its turns: each turn's lines (the timecode line and the lines that wrap from it, each with its break) are a Passage of their own, and the text between the turns (the header, the Agenda items, the motions, the votes, the adjournment, the roster) is cut by `splitPassages` as any text is. Passages are slices of the text (GLOSSARY, Passage), they do not overlap, and they come in the order of the text. The alternative, keeping the whole-text passages and adding the turns beside them, was rejected: a name in a turn would then be held by two Passages, `search` would return the log's lines twice (once as the log's and once as the turn's), and the index's counts and BM25 statistics would count the turn's words twice. With the replacement nothing is lost (the log's other lines are still found, and `get_document` serves the text as published, ADR-0002) and nothing is double-counted.
- **A turn's Passage ends where the page's own lines begin.** A turn's span is its timecode line and the lines that follow it with nothing between; the lines a PDF repeats on every page (the compliance sentence, the committee code, the date, "Page n of m") are not the turn's, so a turn that wraps across a page break has its Passage up to them, and the lines after them are in the text between the turns. The turn's stored speaker still holds both lines. A turn the text does not hold, or whose lines are longer than a Passage may be (510 tokens), has no Passage of its own and its lines are part of the text between the turns: nothing is dropped. Such a turn is counted: the log keeps the number (`speaker_turn_index.turns_without_passage`) and the build report lists each log that has any, with its count and source URL (`recording_log_turns_without_passage`). No log of the fixture has either.
- **A Passage is linked to its turn by the turn's position (schema step 3).** `passage.turn_position` is the turn's position in `speaker_turn` (the log's `document_id` and that position name the turn), and null for every other Passage. Step 3 adds the column and a partial index. The Passages are never embedded: a Recording Log is Keyword-only (ADR-0006), so a turn's Passage has no vector and the vector side never sees it. `versionOneSchema` and step 2 are unchanged.
- **The worker cuts a log from what the store holds, and never fetches to do it.** After the documents step, every stored log that has an index with turns and has not been cut around them (`recordingLogsWithoutTurnPassages`) is cut around its stored turns: the stored text, and each turn's timecode and speaker, in the order of the log, locate the turns in the text (`speakerTurnSpans`: the next block of the text with that timecode whose lines read as that speaker). A log whose links were just read (a new log, or a stored log fetched again by ADR-0052), a log stored before step 3, and a log whose index was stored again (storing an index unlinks the log's Passages, which stay as slices of the text) are all cut by the same step, so there is one way to make a turn's Passage, and a database built under step 2 gets them on its next pull with no request to OLIS.
- **A turn is a Hit, and the Hit says which turn.** `search`'s keyword candidates are Passages, and each turn that matches is a Hit of its own: ranked as `(document, turn)` where any other Passage is ranked as its document, so the log's non-turn lines that match are one more Hit of the log, with no turn. A turn Hit's `id` and `document_id` are the log's (which `get_document` takes), its `snippet` is the turn's lines, and its `turn` field has the speaker, the Legislator Code and Legislator (read from the Legislators the store holds, as `get_document` reads them), the Meeting's id, the Agenda item, the Measure (kept only when the index holds it), the timecode, the second and the player link. Every other Hit has `turn: null`. Both rankings that hold a log (every keyword, and some keywords for a Keyword-only Document) rank turns as they rank Passages of any document; the meaning side never holds one.
- **`get_measure` lists the hearings.** `hearings` has one entry for each Recording Log with an index and each Agenda item of that log that names the Measure: the Meeting (id, committee code, start), the log, the Event ID, the Agenda item as logged and the first turn under it by the recording's clock (then the log's order) with its speaker, Legislator and player link. A hearing is any Agenda item that names the Measure, so a work session is one (HB 3131 has three in the fixture: two hearings and a work session), with its kind and every Measure the item names (see "Amended"). Earliest Meeting first. A Measure no log names has `hearings: []`. A log that belongs to no Meeting the store holds has no entry.

## Amended: the hearings are every Agenda item that names the Measure, and the worker marks what it has done

The owner decided on 2026-11-05 that **a Measure's hearings are every Agenda item naming it**, and the review of the first build asked for the other changes below. Schema step 3 is not released, so it holds all of them.

- **Every item that names the Measure is a hearing.** Public Hearings and Work Sessions both count, each entry carries its `kind` ("Public Hearing", "Work Session", or the item's own wording after its Measures, as in "Informational Meeting"; `hearingKindOf`), and an item with other wording counts too. An Agenda line that names several Measures ("SB 1542 and SB 1543 - Public Hearing", "HB 4077 and HB 4076 A - Public Hearing") is a hearing of each Measure it names. The Measures are the list the line _begins_ with, separated by "and", a comma or "&", each with the letter of an amendment allowed after it (`measuresOfAgendaItem`). A Measure named further into a title ("Nuclear Energy Study (HB 4046, 2026) - Informational Meeting") is not in the list: that line is a topic and not a hearing of that Measure.
- **A turn keeps its first Measure as `measure_id`** (kept only when the index holds it, ADR-0052), and the Agenda item lists them all: each hearing has `measure_ids`, every Measure the item names that the index holds, in the order the line names them.
- **The item's Measures are a table, `speaker_turn_item_measure`** (document, item text, place in the line, Measure id), read by the worker from the stored items of a log that has an index and whose `item_measures_read` is 0. A new log, a log stored before step 3 and a log whose index was stored again are read by the same step, with no fetch, so there is one way to read them. The table keeps a Measure the index does not hold (so it needs no re-reading when the Measure arrives); the reader leaves it out of `measure_ids`. The hearings query joins the table to the turns by the item's text.
- **Within a log, the hearings are ordered by the first turn's second**, then the log's order, and not by the item's text. Across logs: earliest Meeting, committee, log. An item with no turn under it has no first turn and is not listed.
- **A Hit's `turn` and a hearing's `first_turn` carry `offset_source`**, as `get_document`'s turns do.
- **A log is cut once.** `turns_without_passage` is null until the worker has cut the log around its turns and the number of turns left without a Passage after; a log is cut when it is null, so a log of which no turn is placed is not cut again on every pull. Storing the log's index again sets it null again.
- **A turn found by a citation and by the words is one Hit.** The citation path keys a Hit by the turn its passage is, as the keyword path does (`HitKey`).
- **Step 3 is plain `ALTER TABLE`s applied once, in order** (ADR-0048): the test arrangement that makes a file look as it was before versions (`leaveAsBeforeVersions`) removes what step 3 adds, and the step has no branch for a file that already has the column.

## Considered Options

- **Keep the whole-text Passages and add the turns beside them.** Rejected: see above, the same words twice in the index and twice in the results.
- **Make the turn's Passage the turn's speaker text only, apart from the log's text.** Rejected: a Passage is a slice of the Document's text (GLOSSARY), so that what a Hit shows is exactly what the Document says (ADR-0025); the hit's snippet and `get_document`'s text must agree.
- **Store the span of each turn with the turn.** Rejected for now: the spans follow from the stored text and the stored turns, which are in the database already, and a second copy of where a turn is could disagree with the text. The cost is a scan of the log's text when it is cut.
- **A table that links a Passage to a turn, instead of a column.** Rejected: a Passage is one turn's or none, and the column is read in the one query that finds the Passage.
- **Store each turn's item Measures when the log is read, in the turn's row.** Rejected: a database that has the index would need the log fetched again to get them, and the Agenda item's text already holds them.
- **Count only an item's first Measure.** Rejected by the owner (2026-11-05): a hearing on two Measures is a hearing of each.
- **Return one Hit for a log however many of its turns match.** Rejected: "when did this witness speak" is answered by the turn, and a witness may speak more than once in a hearing.
- **Give `get_measure` the log's first turn only, not one for each Agenda item.** Rejected: a log may take a Measure up twice (a hearing and a work session at one Meeting), and each is a place to jump to.

## Consequences

A search that matched a Recording Log before, by the log's words, still finds it: by the Passage the words are in, which is a turn's when they are in a turn. A query that several turns of one log answer now returns one Hit for each, so a log with many matching turns takes more of the `limit` than it did, and the Hits of other documents move down by as many as the extra turn Hits before them. A log's lines are ranked by Passages that are shorter than they were, so BM25's length normalisation weighs them differently from the whole-text Passages'.

A log whose index is stored again (a later ticket's decision about a replaced file, ADR-0052) is cut again on the pull that follows. The turns' Passages and the turns can disagree for the length of one pull, and a Hit whose Passage names a turn the index does not hold is a bug that throws.

The stage-2 transcript passages (spec #123) attach to a turn through the same position and are a decision of their own.
