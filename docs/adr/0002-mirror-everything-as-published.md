# Mirror every document in full, exactly as published, including Testimony

Every document the feed links (Versions, Proposed and Printed Amendments, Fiscal Impact Statements, revenue impact statements, staff measure summaries, agendas, Exhibits, Recording Logs, Testimony) is mirrored as full text, exactly as the Legislature publishes it, with its `source_url` and a notice that it is not the official record. Testimony gets no redaction and no special search rules: it is public material the Legislature already publishes on OLIS.

This deliberately differs from the OregonAI platform, which excluded testimony on privacy grounds. A version that removed contact details and blocked search by a submitter's name was proposed and rejected on 2026-10-29.
